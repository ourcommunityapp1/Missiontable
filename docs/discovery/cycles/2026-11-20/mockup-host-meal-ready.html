December’s meal, ready to send

Opportunity 1 (discovery mockup, cycle 2026-11-20). The monthly rhythm stopped when the host stopped sending meals. Today that job starts from a blank 9-field “New kit” form, every month. In this version Mission Table drafts the meal ten days ahead: a dish from the country, a short passage, and prayer requests (from the field update if there is one, otherwise from the country’s prayer points). The host reads it, adds a line, and sends. A missed month is a rest, not a debt.

All content here is sample data for a test. It is not production copy, and none of it is from a real member.
